About the Investment Projects Portal

Are you a project promoter looking to reach potential investors? Register your project on the Investment Projects Portal and give it visibility to a network of investors. Submitting a project is free and takes only a few minutes.

Are you an investor looking for investment opportunities across renewable energy, infrastructure, technology, or SME financing? The Investment Projects Portal gives you a curated list of approved projects, with filters to find the ones that match your interests, and a direct way to contact the promoters behind them.
